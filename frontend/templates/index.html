Built for Healthcare Navigation, Not Diagnosis

Care Connect enforces rigorous safety boundaries at every stage.

Zero Medical Diagnosis

AI categorizes symptoms solely to direct you to the appropriate medical field (e.g. Skin $\rightarrow$ Dermatology).

Zero Drug Prescriptions

Care Connect never prescribes medications. Prescriptions must be issued by certified doctors during consultation.

Emergency Redirection
